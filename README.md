# NJMD Health — Preventive Health Resources

Curated wellness guides and preventive care resources for adult patients in South Jersey, provided by [NJMD Health](https://njmdhealth.com).

## About NJMD Health

NJMD Health is a board-certified internal medicine practice in Marlton, New Jersey, led by Dr. Nimra Rana. We specialize in adult primary care, chronic disease management, and preventive wellness, delivering personalized, physician-led healthcare for South Jersey communities.

  - - **Chronic Disease Management** — Blood pressure, diabetes, metabolic health

                - ## Preventive Health Checklist for Adults
               
                - ### Annual
                - - [ ] Comprehensive physical exam
                  - [ ] - [ ] Blood pressure check
                  - [ ] - [ ] Cholesterol panel (lipid profile)
                  - [ ] - [ ] Blood glucose / A1C screening
                  - [ ] - [ ] BMI assessment
                 
                  - [ ] ### Age-Based Screenings
                  - [ ] - [ ] Colonoscopy (starting at age 45)

                  - [ ] - [ ] Skin cancer screening
                  - [ ] - [ ] Bone density scan (as recommended)
                 
                  - [ ] ### Immunizations
                  - [ ] - [ ] Annual flu vaccine

                  - [ ] - [ ] Shingles vaccine (age 50+)
                  - [ ] - [ ] Pneumonia vaccine (age 65+)
                  - [ ] - [ ] Tdap booster (every 10 years)
